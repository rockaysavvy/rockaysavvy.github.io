The DE 44 Elite is an unlockable legendary hand cannon. It comprises a heavily modified DE 44 with a red dot sight which offers marginally boosted performance, in the same class as the Swiss Elite ES. It’s the best unlockable hand cannon due to its superb reload speed, overall damage output, and handling.

Appearances

  • After players unlock the weapon in multiplayer, the Arms Dealer may sell DE 44 Elites to respected, formidable, godfather, and crime boss players in Baker’s Battle. Respected, formidable, godfather, and crime boss players can also purchase DE 44 Elites from the black market.

Tuna

“You recall the doorman at the King’s party?”

This article is unfinished. It may contain incomplete or inaccurate information.

Tuna is a unique character in Baker’s crew and one of the only survivors of the party explosion. Before Baker began his campaign to take over the city, he worked as a bouncer at King’s skyscraper and a number of other high-class establishments in Rockay.
